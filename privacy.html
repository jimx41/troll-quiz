2. Advertising

The App is supported by advertising served through Google AdMob. Depending on your region and consent choices, you may see:

  • Personalized (targeted) ads — based on your advertising ID and interests inferred by Google and its advertising partners.
  • Non-personalized ads — shown without using your advertising ID for interest-based targeting, based only on limited contextual information such as general location and device type.

Where required by law (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we display a consent form before showing personalized ads, and you may choose non-personalized ads or withdraw consent at any time from the App's settings.

Subscribers to the Live Vault paid tier never see ads of any kind. Ad data described in this section applies only to free-tier use of the App.

How to control ad personalization

  • In the App: open Settings → Ad Preferences (or the consent prompt shown on first launch) to change your choice at any time.
  • On your device: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of ads personalization."
  • Google-wide: visit adssettings.google.com.

AdMob's own data practices are described at Google's Partner Sites policy.
